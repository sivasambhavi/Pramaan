## VALEDICTORY FUNCTION OF NATIONAL INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY PUDUCHERRY

###

Posted On:
15 MAR 2024 8:09PM by PIB Chennai

Chennai, March 15, 2024

The valedictory function of Annual Sports Day (Zenith 24) of National Institute of Technology Puducherry held on 15.03.2024 at 04:00 PM. The Chief Guest Comdt. Vijay Viswanathan, Commandant, Coast Guard, Karaikal has distributed the prizes and medals to the winners and runners in the presence of Dr. S. Sundaravarathan, Registrar, NIT Puducherry and Dr. Sendhil Kumar N, Dean (Students Welfare), NIT Puducherry. The Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering (EEE) has won the Overall Trophy of Zenith 24 (Annual Sports Meet).

All the necessary arrangements made by Dr. S. Babu, Senior Student Activity and Sports Officer (Senior SASO) and Team, Mr. R. Ganesh Kumar, Sports Secretary and Students of NIT Puducherry. All the Faculty, Staff and Students have participated in this event.
